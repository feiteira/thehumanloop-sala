# Glossary

| English | Português | Español | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Folder | Pasta | Carpeta | Everything about one patient. |
| Shelf | Estante | Estantería | All your folders; the archive holds closed ones. |
| Page | Página | Página | The record of one session. |
| Sticky note | Nota adesiva | Nota adhesiva | A short reminder on a cover or a page. |
| Peel off | Descolar | Despegar | Mark a sticky note as done. |
| Divider | Separador | Separador | A section of a folder: Pages, Themes, Calendar, Documents, Notes history. |
| Theme | Tema | Tema | A word or two you mark on passages of your notes, to follow it session by session. |
| Private process note | Nota de processo privada | Nota de proceso privada | Your own reflections, kept apart from the record. |
| Passphrase | Frase-passe | Frase de acceso | What you type to open Sala. |
| Recovery phrase | Frase de recuperação | Frase de recuperación | 12 words that open your practice if you forget the passphrase. |
| Backup | Cópia de segurança | Copia de seguridad | An encrypted copy of the whole practice, in a folder you choose. |
