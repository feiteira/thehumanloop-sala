# Sala — user manual

Sala keeps your practice on your computer: a folder for each patient, a page for each session, sticky notes, and your calendar. Everything is encrypted and nothing is sent anywhere.

1. [Getting started](01-getting-started.md) — installing Sala, the example practice, your passphrase and recovery phrase, backups, opening and locking, keyboard shortcuts and Find
2. [Folders, pages and sticky notes](02-folders-pages-notes.md) — the shelf and searching your notes, the folder cover, writing session pages and templates, sticky notes, documents, archiving and deleting
3. [Your calendar](03-calendar.md) — the Today view and notes still to write, appointments, repeating sessions and changing one date, booking the next session, working hours
4. [Bringing your practice with you](04-bringing-your-practice.md) — importing folders, documents, scans, patient lists and calendars
5. [Your data](05-your-data.md) — backups, recently deleted, exporting, moving to a new computer, what leaves this device, giving a patient a copy of their folder
6. [Questions and troubleshooting](06-questions.md) — a forgotten passphrase, a failed backup, Sala locking itself
7. [Glossary](glossary.md)
